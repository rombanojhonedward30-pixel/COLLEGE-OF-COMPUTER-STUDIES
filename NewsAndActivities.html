ACADEMIC SEMINAR

October 15, 2026

The College of Computer Studies (CCS) and the Association of Computer Enthusiasts Students (ACES) invites all CCS students to attend the upcoming CCS Seminar 2026. This seminar aims to provide valuable insights into the latest trends and developments in the field of computer studies, as well as to enhance students' knowledge and skills.CCS SEMINAR 2026: Empowering Future IT Professionals The College of Computer Studies (CCS), in partnership with the Association of Computer Enthusiasts Students (ACES), will hold the CCS Seminar 2026 on October 15, 2026, from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM at the CTC Multimedia Room. With the theme “Empowering Future IT Professionals: Artificial Intelligence, Career Opportunities, and Skills for the Digital Age,” the seminar aims to help students understand the rapidly evolving world of technology and prepare for future careers in the IT industry. The event will feature speakers who will share their knowledge and experiences about artificial intelligence, emerging technologies, and various career opportunities in the field of information technology.

More than just a seminar, this event is an opportunity for students to discover new ideas, develop useful skills, ask questions, and gain inspiration for their future careers. As technology continues to advance, acquiring relevant skills and staying open to new opportunities are essential for success in the digital age. All CCS students are encouraged to participate and make the most of this valuable learning experience. Be part of CCS Seminar 2026! Let’s learn together, strengthen our skills, and take another step toward a brighter future in the world of technology.

We encourage all CCS students to take part in this exciting seminar and make the most of this opportunity to learn something new. This is a chance to gain useful knowledge, discover new career possibilities, and better understand how technology and artificial intelligence can shape our future. So, mark your calendars, invite your classmates, and come ready to learn and share ideas. Let’s take this opportunity to grow, improve our skills, and prepare ourselves for the future of IT. See you at CCS Seminar 2027!

Congratulations to our Seminar Participants!

Teacher's Day Celebration

October 5, 2026

Teacher’s Day Celebration: A Day of Appreciation and Gratitude On October 3, 2026, students came together to celebrate Teacher’s Day and thank the teachers who guide and support them. It was a meaningful chance to recognize their patience, dedication, and hard work.

Teachers do more than teach lessons. They offer advice, encourage students, and help prepare them for the future. The celebration reminded everyone that even simple gestures and heartfelt messages can make teachers feel valued.

The event also brought students and teachers closer, creating happy memories and strengthening their bond. It highlighted the importance of appreciating those who help students learn, grow, and reach their goals.

As the celebration ended, students were reminded to show respect and gratitude not only on special occasions but every day. Happy Teacher’s Day to all our hardworking teachers! Thank you for your guidance, patience, and inspiration.

Happy Teacher's Day!

× PINNING

ACADEMIC SEMINAR

October 8, 2026

In a significant step toward their professional careers, students of Ceguera Technological Colleges, Inc. (CTCI) gathered for their formal Pinning Ceremony, held under the canopy of the Julio D. Ceguera Founders Stage. The event celebrated the dedication, hard work, and achievements of a new group of student-professionals as they prepared to advance to the next phase of their training. It marked an important milestone in their academic journey and recognized their readiness to apply their knowledge and skills in their respective fields.

Joined by members of the faculty and administration, including academic leaders dressed in traditional Filipiniana attire, the students gathered for a commemorative photo following the official pinning ceremony. The occasion was filled with pride, gratitude, and excitement as the CTCI community celebrated the students' accomplishments and supported their professional growth. The ceremony served as a reminder of the importance of dedication, responsibility, and perseverance in achieving future career goals.

Congratulations!

DEPARTMENT BACKGROUND

The College of Computer Studies (CCS) focuses on developing students' knowledge and technical skills in computer systems, programming, networking, database management, web development, and information systems. It provides practical learning experiences that prepare students to apply technology in solving real-world problems and meeting the needs of modern industries.
